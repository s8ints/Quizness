1. Learn: the accounting equation

Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s equity. Subtract liabilities from assets to find owner’s equity. For example, assets of BBD 900 and liabilities of BBD 300 give equity of BBD 600.

Concept reference: OpenStax, Principles of Accounting, section 2.3. The practice numbers and questions below are illustrative examples written for this prototype.

2. Practise: balance the books

A small business has assets of BBD 1,200 and liabilities of BBD 450. What is its owner’s equity?

BBD 750: correct

1,200 − 450 = 750. Check it: 450 + 750 = 1,200, so the equation balances.

3. Retry: apply the same concept

Another business has assets of BBD 800 and owner’s equity of BBD 500. What are its liabilities?

  • BBD 300
  • BBD 1,300

BBD 300: correct

800 − 500 = 300. Check: 300 + 500 = 800.

View a sample progress report.

BBD 1,300: try again

Adding would make liabilities exceed the assets while equity is positive. Subtract equity from assets: 800 − 500.
