For a course partner or pilot sponsor

Help us test one course well.

Our ask is access to a consenting cohort, two course activity sets, a peer-support coordinator and a lecturer reviewer for a four-week pilot. We would agree a modest budget after estimating development, review and support costs.

At the end, we would report repeat use, performance on separate application tasks and delivery costs, then decide whether to continue.

Name research

On 9 October 2026, searches for “Quizzness” and “Quizzness company” returned similar quiz-related wording, including “Risky Quizzness” event listings and “Quizzness as usual” quiz nights. These are not claimed as our venture or inspiration for its artwork.

We retain the owner’s chosen Quizzness name provisionally. The search does not establish exclusive ownership or originality of the name; a fuller name check remains a launch task.

The supplied group proposal flags a possible same-name trivia platform at quizzness.com. Its live page could not be verified during this check. The name remains provisional pending investigation of that reported conflict. [9]
