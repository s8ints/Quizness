Survey results

Student Learning & Study Experience Survey: 28 submitted responses, supplied by the Quizzness project team. Counts below were calculated directly from the supplied CSV.

Percentages use all 28 responses and are rounded to one decimal place. Questions 2, 4, 7 and 12 allow multiple selections, so their percentages can total more than 100%.

Recruitment, location and respondent eligibility are not documented in the file. These responses describe this sample, not all students or the Barbados market. Interest after reading a favourable concept description does not establish actual use, willingness to pay or improved learning.
