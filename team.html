Venture status: Quizzness is a university startup proposal and front-end prototype. No launched service, university partnership, or GiveBack recognition is claimed.

A proposed learning business

Quizzness would turn university coursework into short game-based learning activities, connect students to useful explanations, and help them find screened peer tutors. Universities and colleges would be potential institutional customers, while students and peer tutors would use the platform.

Our proposed mission

Help university students practise difficult concepts, understand their mistakes, and find an appropriate next step when they need support.

The problem we aim to solve

Following a classroom explanation does not always translate into independent practice. Students may struggle to identify a mistake or arrange timely help through informal study groups and chats.

Our proposed connection is coursework → practice → feedback → peer support → retry. Tutor GiveBack time would require session confirmation, coordinator review, and a formal university agreement.

Our next step

We would like a university partner or sponsor to help us test a four-week pilot in two courses. We would need access to course material, a content reviewer and approved peer tutors.

We would agree a budget after estimating development and support costs. After the pilot, we would review student use, feedback, tutoring workload and the cost of continuing.
