How hours become recognised

  1. The tutor and learner confirm the session record.
  2. An authorised coordinator checks programme eligibility and time.
  3. Approved time moves to the verified balance.
  4. A report or certificate is issued under the partner’s rules.

University recognition is required. Quizzness cannot promise that logged time meets a service-hour requirement without a formal agreement.

Interested in becoming a peer tutor?

The proposed application checks course completion, minimum academic results, coordinator approval, training and the code of conduct. Applications and certificate downloads are not implemented in this prototype.
