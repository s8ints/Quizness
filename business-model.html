Quizness freemium model, value creation, delivery, capture, and model comparison.

04 / THE BUSINESS MODEL

Shared value.
A sustainable study circle.

A proposed standard freemium model with a community contribution loop. The differentiation is the course-specific content and review process.

CREATE VALUE

Knowledge that compounds

Students contribute relevant practice. Proposed peer review helps identify unclear questions and improve explanations.

DELIVER VALUE

Meet learners in their course

Deliver short practice sessions through course circles, topic collections, and peer tutor participation.

CAPTURE VALUE

Free to start. Optional extras.

Core practice stays free in this concept. An imagined premium plan adds personal practice collections and progress summaries.

BDS $8 / month Fictional price

Classification: a standard freemium subscription model. A contribution network is a proposed source of differentiation; no evidence currently supports calling Quizness disruptive.

MODEL COMPARISON

A familiar model, a course-specific focus.

Apply the course concepts of a standard versus disruptive model and value creation, delivery, and capture.

Compared with paid-only access

Freemium lowers the initial barrier to joining a course circle. Paid-only access could generate revenue earlier but may reduce participation and contributions. Quizness depends on validating conversion after useful free practice.

Compared with advertising

A subscription ties revenue to optional learner benefits. An advertising model would require advertiser demand and sufficient traffic; it may also compete with focused study time.

Relevant external example

Quizlet offers free access alongside subscription options. Quizness proposes the same broad revenue pattern, with a course-circle review workflow. This does not establish a unique competitive advantage.

ECONOMIC ASSUMPTIONS

Review effort is part of the cost.

Illustrative price: BDS $8 per month, subject to willingness-to-pay testing.

Revenue driver

Monthly revenue = paying learners × BDS $8. A sample 10 paying learners would generate BDS $80 before expenses; this is arithmetic, not a forecast.

Cost drivers

Hosting, support, payment fees, tutor review, and community moderation. Volunteer effort still has a time cost and cannot be assumed to scale for free.

Go / revise decision

Estimate contribution per paying learner after variable costs. Revise pricing or scope if realistic conversion cannot cover review and recurring operations.
