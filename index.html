02 / PROBLEM & INTENDED USERS

Bring learning and support into one place.

Peer tutors already exist in schools, but a clear structure does not always connect them. Students can also struggle to learn and apply concepts independently and need engaging, additional assistance.

Fragmented support

Students may rely on informal WhatsApp chats or separate study groups instead of one organised place to find screened peer tutors.

Difficulty applying concepts

Understanding coursework in class does not always translate into applying it alone. Games and guided practice offer another opportunity to work through the material.

Feedback and help arrive late

The team identifies weak feedback, delayed assistance and loss of concentration as problems that can interrupt independent study.

These are the team’s confirmed problem statements and supporting observations. Interview findings and external citations for their prevalence still need to be added.

Who Quizzness serves

Students

Learn coursework, test retained information through games, receive feedback and practise weaknesses with optional peer help.

Peer tutors

Provide screened and verified support and earn GiveBack hours where an institution recognises the programme.

Universities & colleges

Connect students to structured peer support and provide access through the primary B2B2C model.

Official GiveBack recognition requires institutional approval and verification. The prototype does not claim an existing partnership.

03 / WHY NOW

Connect practice to the next useful action.

The concept brings interactive course learning and peer support into one journey. Start with one university, Programming and Mathematics, then test whether learners return and tutors can provide reliable help.

Existing tools already provide practice and social study: see the industry analysis and linked product sources. The proposed opportunity is the transition from a specific weak topic to course-approved peer help. Its value remains unvalidated.
