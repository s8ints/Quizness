The learning loop

Every attempt has a next step.

  1. 01

    Learn

    Read the course material.

  2. 02

    Take a quest

    Try a game or practice question.

  3. 03

    Reflect

    Check the feedback.

  4. 04

    Find support

    Practise the weak topic or ask a peer tutor for help.

  5. 05

    Try again

    Return to the idea and give it another try.

A demonstration, not a live service. This prototype shows two practice questions and a tutoring example. It does not create accounts, save scores, or book sessions. GiveBack recognition would require a formal university agreement.

The opportunity

Bridge the gap
between class and confidence.

Following an explanation in class and answering a question on your own are different things. We want to help students practise between classes and find someone to explain topics they are struggling with.

Study groups and WhatsApp chats can be useful, but our idea puts practice, feedback and peer support in one place.

“An opportunity is a favorable set of circumstances that creates a need for a new product, service, or business.”

(Barringer & Ireland, 2019, Ch. 2, p. 72)

An early signal

22 of 28

students in our survey (78.6%) said they have difficulty understanding course topics at least sometimes.

A small exploratory survey, not proof of demand. We still need interviews to find out whether students would keep using Quizzness.
