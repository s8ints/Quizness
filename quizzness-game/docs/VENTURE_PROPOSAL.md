# Group proposal — supplied context

Proposed slogan: **Quizzness Over Pleasure**. Guild identity and Panthy the Thinking Purple Panther belong to the learning experience.

Proposed pitch: Quizzness transforms courses and subjects into interactive games and activities, with a future connection to screened and verified peer tutors who can earn give-back hours. This is venture intent, not a claim that tutoring, tutor screening or recognised hours already operate.

Intended users are tertiary students who need engaging help learning and applying concepts. The proposed problems include fragmented tutoring, delayed feedback/help, informal WhatsApp support and difficulty maintaining concentration. These are team hypotheses; no research evidence or citations were supplied to substantiate them.

Core loop: coursework → games/activities → useful feedback → practice weaknesses → retry. Saving progress and engaging repetition support this loop. Tutors, reviews and give-back verification belong to Phase 6; institutional recognition requires agreement.

Proposed business model: B2B2C, with possible premium student subscriptions and tutoring commissions. These are hypotheses, not payment features or committed pricing. Proposed competitors/substitutes include Duolingo, Quizlet, informal groups and in-person study groups. Universities, colleges, lecturers and students influence adoption.
