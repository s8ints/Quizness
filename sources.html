Updated student survey findings

The updated group report summarises 30 anonymous responses collected on 8–10 October 2026. Percentages below use 30 as the denominator and are rounded to one decimal place. Multiple-selection totals can exceed 100%.

The survey spans several education levels and is not a representative sample of all university students. Recruitment and response verification are not documented. Only the aggregate findings supplied in the updated report are shown; a full response-by-response dataset and individual written answers are not published here.
