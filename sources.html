Written feedback and next steps

Q21 has 19 nonblank entries and nine blanks; “I don’t know” and “Unsure” count as nonblank. Comments suggest testing course relevance, clear explanations, useful feedback, visible progress and affordability. These are qualitative inputs, not coded theme frequencies.

Next, test actual return use, the cost of reviewed content and tutor coordination, and institutional willingness to fund access.
